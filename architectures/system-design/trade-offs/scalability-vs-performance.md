## Scalability vs performance

### Scalability

- System’s ability to handle increasing loads (more users, more data) without a significant drop in service quality (capacity to grow and maintain efficient operation)
- Enhancing scalability may lead to reduced performance due to increased overheads like network latency and data synchronization
- Approach through horizontal or vertical scaling

### Performance

- Refers to how efficiently the system handles requests (e.g., speed and throughput) under a specific load
- Optimizing algorithms, reducing bottlenecks, increasing throughput
