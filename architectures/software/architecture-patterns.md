## Software architecture patterns

These are high-level design strategies that define how software systems should be structured and how components should interact. They guide:

- Separation of concerns
- Dependency management
- Maintainability and scalability
- Testability

Following are some guidelines when applying patterns to your codebase:

- Don't overengineer. Avoid introducing complexity or abstractions unless they solve a real problem
- Know when to violate rules. Architecture is guide, be pragmatic, not dogmatic
- Let the domain drive the design. Structure your system based on core business needs, not frameworks or tools
- Keep boundaries explicit, not abstract. Use clear, minimal interfaces between layers
- Design for change, prioritize modularity and readability over idealism
- Let the team agree on the rules. Shared understanding is better than enforced purity
